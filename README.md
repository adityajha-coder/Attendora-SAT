## About The Project

Attendora is a comprehensive attendance tracking and academic management platform meticulously designed to help students stay on top of their classes effortlessly. For many students, balancing lectures, assignments, and personal life can lead to missed classes and falling behind on academic requirements. Attendora bridges this gap by providing powerful insights, intuitive goal tracking, and automated schedule management. By simplifying the academic experience, Attendora allows students to focus entirely on learning rather than worrying about the logistics of their daily schedule.

### Why Attendora?

Managing university life is overwhelming. Between remembering which classes you have attended, calculating how many more you can afford to miss, and keeping track of assignments, it is easy to lose focus. Attendora centralizes your academic life into one beautiful, lightning fast Progressive Web App. With offline support, AI powered schedule scanning, and seamless cloud synchronization, it acts as your personal academic assistant.

## Technology Stack

Attendora is built using modern web technologies to ensure a fast, responsive, and reliable experience across all devices.

* **Frontend Structure:** Pure HTML5 for semantic and accessible layout building.
* **Styling and Animations:** Vanilla CSS3 enhanced with modern layout techniques and smooth micro animations.
* **Logic and Interactivity:** Vanilla JavaScript for a lightweight, dependency free frontend architecture.
* **Backend and Database:** Supabase for real time cloud synchronization, secure authentication, and robust data storage.
* **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API for intelligently parsing timetable images.
* **Architecture:** Fully compliant Progressive Web App, meaning it can be installed on any device and works entirely offline.

## Comprehensive Features

### Smart Attendance Tracking
At the core of Attendora is a robust attendance tracking system. You can easily monitor your presence across various subjects and clinical rotations. The system calculates your overall attendance percentage in real time, giving you a clear picture of where you stand in each course. 

### Advanced Goal Setting
Setting goals is crucial for academic success. Attendora allows you to set custom attendance goals for individual classes. The platform then provides actionable insights, telling you exactly how many classes you can afford to miss or how many consecutive classes you need to attend to reach your target percentage. The "What If" calculator provides immediate feedback on how future absences will impact your standing.

### Automated Schedule Scanning
Say goodbye to manual data entry. Attendora features an AI powered timetable scanner. You can simply upload an image of your schedule, and the system will automatically parse the subjects, timings, and days, directly importing them into your dashboard.

### Secure Cloud Synchronization
Never worry about losing your data. Attendora offers seamless cloud synchronization. By signing in, your schedule, attendance history, notes, and assignments are securely backed up to the cloud. You can access your academic profile from any device, anywhere, and anytime.

### Gamification and Streaks
To keep you motivated, Attendora incorporates gamification elements. Maintain attendance streaks by showing up to your scheduled classes day after day. At the end of a semester, you can view a comprehensive summary of your performance, highlighting your most attended classes and overall dedication.

### Integrated Assignment Management
Academic success is not just about showing up. The integrated assignment tracker allows you to record upcoming deadlines, essays, and projects in one unified dashboard. Keep your coursework organized alongside your daily schedule.

### Real Time GPA Calculator
Estimate your academic performance comprehensively. The built in GPA calculator works hand in hand with your attendance metrics, allowing you to project your final grades based on your current standing and credit hours.

## How To Use The Platform

1. **Dashboard Overview** 
Upon entering the application, you are greeted by the main dashboard. This view prioritizes your upcoming classes for the current day. You can quickly mark yourself as present, absent, or note if a class was cancelled directly from this main view.

2. **Managing Your Schedule** 
Navigate to the "My Courses" section to take control of your academic timeline. Here you can add new classes, edit existing ones, or adjust instructor and room details. This is also where you can utilize the AI scanner to upload your timetable.

3. **Setting Goals and What If Scenarios** 
Clicking on any course card opens a detailed view. Within this view, you can set your desired attendance goal. Utilize the "What If" calculator to simulate future absences or attendances, helping you make informed decisions about skipping a lecture.

4. **Interactive Calendar View** 
The calendar tab provides a monthly visual representation of your attendance history. Days with scheduled classes are marked with indicators. Clicking on any specific date will reveal a detailed breakdown of your classes for that day, your recorded status, and any custom notes you have attached to that record.

5. **Adding Notes and Reasons** 
For meticulous record keeping, you can add custom notes to any attendance record. If you are absent, you can specify a reason or leave a reminder to get notes from a classmate. These details are stored and displayed within the calendar view for future reference.

6. **Customizing Your Experience** 
Personalize your viewing experience by toggling between dark and light themes in the Settings menu. You can also edit your user profile, change your display name, and manage your cloud sync preferences.
